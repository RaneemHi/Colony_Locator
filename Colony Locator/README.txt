Colony Locator

Colony Locator is a simple browser-based tool for quickly identifying colony positions from grid images generated by Iris.

Upload a `.grid.jpg` image together with its corresponding `.iris` file. You can then click directly on a colony to identify its position (e.g. A5 or AA32) and view the associated Iris measurements.

You can also enter a position to locate and highlight that colony on the plate.

Features

* Click a colony to identify its row and column position
* Search for a position (e.g. `AA32`) and highlight it on the plate
* Display colony size, circularity and opacity from the `.iris` file
* Select and save multiple colony positions
* Export selected colonies as a CSV file
* Supports different grid dimensions based on the uploaded Iris data
* Runs entirely within your browser — plate images and Iris files are not uploaded to a server

Using Colony Locator

1. Upload your `.grid.jpg` image.
2. Upload the corresponding `.iris` file.
3. Click a colony to identify its position, or enter a position in Find a position.
4. Add colonies of interest to the selected list.
5. Export your selections as a CSV if required.

Privacy

All files are processed locally within your browser. Colony Locator does not upload your plate images or `.iris` files to a server.
